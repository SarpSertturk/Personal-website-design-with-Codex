EROSION OF CRITICAL THINKING IN THE AGE OF AI 
 
    Technological advancements have shaped the way people interact with the environment.  
From the most primitive tools to the most advanced technologies, humanity's spatial 
understanding has been transformed by these technologies which led to changes in interactions 
between people and the environment. This technology-powered change enabled to increase in 
the efficiency and effectiveness of the processes by enabling reduced human errors, a decrease 
in lead time, better forecasts, enhanced data-driven decision-making, etc. Especially with the 
help of the new Generative AI tools, the usage of information has reached another level. Despite 
these benefits, there are side effects due to the overreliance on AI models during research, 
decision-making, and other intellectual efforts which could lead to the erosion of critical 
thinking by decreasing intellectual skepticism,  inference-making capabilities from the data, 
awareness of multiple perspectives, comparison of the  knowledge sources, hypothesis 
development ability to address the problem, and context understanding. This cognitive decline 
could also slow analytical & creative thinking capabilities which are crucial to structure & solve 
complex and multidimensional problems. In the light of discussion above, the issue of 
overreliance on AI models needs to be considered to ensure intellectual capital development 
and maintain the reasoning skills that are necessary for innovation. This consideration process 
revolves around two dimensions which are learning and ethics.

framework which encompasses data, information, knowledge, and wisdom layers . The 
relationship between those components is shown below.
